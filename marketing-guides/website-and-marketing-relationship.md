# Why Websites and Digital Marketing Work Together

## Overview

Many businesses invest in marketing without having a strong website.

A website and digital marketing strategy should work together.

## Website Role

* Builds credibility
* Collects enquiries
* Provides business information
* Supports conversions

## Marketing Role

* Drives traffic
* Increases visibility
* Generates awareness
* Attracts potential customers

## Example

A Google Ads campaign can bring visitors to a website.

The website then converts visitors into enquiries through forms, calls, or WhatsApp.

## Benefits of Combining Both

* Better lead generation
* Improved return on investment
* Increased customer trust
* Higher conversion rates

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can marketing work without a website?

It can, but results are often limited.

### Why do businesses need both?

Marketing attracts visitors while websites convert visitors into customers.
