# Google Ads for Small Businesses

## Overview

Google Ads allows businesses to appear when potential customers search for relevant services.

## Benefits

* Immediate visibility
* Local targeting
* Budget control
* Measurable results

## Common Campaign Types

### Search Ads

Appear in Google Search results.

### Display Ads

Appear across websites within Google's advertising network.

### Remarketing Ads

Reconnect with previous website visitors.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Google Ads suitable for small businesses?

Yes. Campaigns can be launched with flexible budgets.

### How quickly can results be seen?

Traffic and enquiries may begin shortly after campaigns are approved.
