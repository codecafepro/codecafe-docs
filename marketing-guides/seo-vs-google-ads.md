# SEO vs Google Ads

## Overview

Businesses often ask whether they should invest in SEO or Google Ads.

Both strategies have different objectives and timelines.

## SEO

### Benefits

* Long-term visibility
* Organic traffic
* Increased credibility

### Considerations

* Results take time
* Requires ongoing optimization

## Google Ads

### Benefits

* Immediate visibility
* Faster lead generation
* Highly targeted campaigns

### Considerations

* Requires advertising budget
* Traffic stops when campaigns stop

## Recommended Strategy

Many businesses achieve the best results by combining SEO and Google Ads.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which is better for a new business?

Google Ads can provide faster visibility while SEO builds long-term growth.

### Can SEO and Ads work together?

Yes. Many businesses use both strategies simultaneously.
