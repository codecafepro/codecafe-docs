# What is Digital Marketing?

## Overview

Digital marketing is the process of promoting products and services using online channels such as search engines, websites, social media platforms, email marketing, and paid advertising.

Businesses use digital marketing to increase visibility, generate leads, and attract customers.

## Common Digital Marketing Channels

### Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

Improving website visibility on Google Search.

### Google Ads

Paid advertising on Google Search and Display Network.

### Social Media Marketing

Marketing through platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and YouTube.

### Email Marketing

Communicating with existing and potential customers through email campaigns.

### Content Marketing

Creating valuable content to educate and attract customers.

## Benefits of Digital Marketing

* Increased brand awareness
* Better customer targeting
* Measurable results
* Lead generation
* Improved business growth

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is digital marketing important for small businesses?

Yes. Digital marketing helps small businesses compete with larger organizations by reaching customers online.

### Can digital marketing generate leads?

Yes. When properly planned, digital marketing can consistently generate enquiries and sales opportunities.
