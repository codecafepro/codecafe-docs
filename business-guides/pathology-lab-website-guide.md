# Why Pathology Labs Need a Website

## Overview

A pathology website helps laboratories improve visibility, patient communication, and service accessibility.

## Benefits

### Test Information

Patients can view available tests.

### Home Collection Requests

Patients can request sample collection.

### Better Search Visibility

Appearing in Google Search increases enquiries.

### Digital Trust

Patients prefer labs with professional online presence.

## Recommended Features

* Test Catalogue
* Home Collection Form
* Contact Details
* WhatsApp Integration
* Google Maps
* Report Download Section
* FAQs

## Common Questions

### Can pathology labs get leads from websites?

Yes. Many patients search online for nearby labs.

### Is WhatsApp integration useful?

Yes. It simplifies communication and enquiry handling.
