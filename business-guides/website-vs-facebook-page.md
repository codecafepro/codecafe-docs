# Website vs Facebook Page: Which Does Your Business Need?

## Overview

Many business owners wonder whether a Facebook page is enough or if they also need a website.

The truth is both serve different purposes.

## Facebook Page Benefits

* Quick to create
* Easy customer engagement
* Social sharing
* Advertising opportunities

## Website Benefits

* Full ownership
* Professional credibility
* Better Google visibility
* Lead generation
* Detailed business information
* Long-term digital asset

## Comparison

### Ownership

Facebook Page:
Owned by Meta

Website:
Owned by the business

### Search Visibility

Facebook Page:
Limited

Website:
Strong SEO opportunities

### Branding

Facebook Page:
Restricted design

Website:
Complete branding control

### Lead Generation

Facebook Page:
Basic

Website:
Advanced forms, booking, WhatsApp, landing pages

## Recommended Approach

Businesses should use both.

The website acts as the primary digital asset while social media supports engagement and marketing.
