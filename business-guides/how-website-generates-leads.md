# How a Website Generates Business Leads

## Overview

A website is more than an online brochure. It can actively generate enquiries and customers.

## Lead Generation Methods

### Contact Forms

Visitors can submit enquiries.

### WhatsApp Integration

Customers can instantly connect with the business.

### Call Buttons

Mobile users can call directly.

### Google Search Traffic

SEO helps attract relevant visitors.

### Landing Pages

Dedicated pages improve campaign performance.

## Benefits

* Increased Enquiries
* Better Customer Trust
* Improved Conversion Rates
* Measurable Results
