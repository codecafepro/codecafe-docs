# Why Clinics Need a Professional Website

## Overview

Patients increasingly search online before booking appointments or visiting healthcare providers.

A clinic website helps establish trust and improve patient communication.

## Benefits of a Clinic Website

### Doctor Profiles

Patients can learn about doctors and specializations.

### Appointment Requests

Patients can request appointments online.

### Location Visibility

Google Maps integration helps patients find the clinic.

### Mobile Accessibility

Most healthcare searches now happen on mobile devices.

### Improved Trust

A professional online presence builds confidence.

## Recommended Features

* Doctor Profiles
* Services
* Appointment Form
* Contact Information
* Google Maps
* WhatsApp Integration
* Testimonials
* Frequently Asked Questions

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does every clinic need a website?

Yes. A website helps patients discover and contact the clinic.

### Can a clinic generate patient enquiries online?

Yes. Websites can generate appointment requests and enquiries.
