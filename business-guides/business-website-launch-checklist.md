# Business Website Launch Checklist

## Before Going Live

### Content Review

* Business Name
* Services
* Contact Information
* Email Address
* Phone Number

### Technical Review

* SSL Enabled
* Mobile Responsive
* Contact Forms Working
* WhatsApp Working

### SEO Review

* Meta Titles
* Meta Descriptions
* Sitemap
* Google Search Console

### User Experience Review

* Fast Loading
* Clear Navigation
* Visible Contact Information

### Security Review

* Website Backup
* Firewall
* Security Plugins

## Post Launch

* Submit Website to Google
* Connect Google Analytics
* Create Google Business Profile
* Start Content Marketing
