# Startup Website Checklist

## Overview

A website is often the first impression a startup creates.

Before launching, ensure the following elements are included.

## Essential Pages

* Home
* About Us
* Services
* Contact
* Privacy Policy

## Trust Elements

* Company Information
* Founder Information
* Testimonials
* Client Logos
* Contact Details

## Lead Generation

* Contact Form
* WhatsApp Integration
* Call Button

## SEO Basics

* Meta Titles
* Meta Descriptions
* Sitemap
* Mobile Optimization

## Technical Requirements

* SSL Certificate
* Secure Hosting
* Fast Loading Speed

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does every startup need a website?

Yes. A website improves credibility and supports customer acquisition.

### Can startups begin with a simple website?

Absolutely. Many successful startups begin with a basic business website and scale later.
