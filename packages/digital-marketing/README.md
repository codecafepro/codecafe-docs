## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does digital marketing take to show results?

Digital marketing results depend on competition, industry, strategy, and goals. SEO typically requires long-term consistency, while paid campaigns can generate faster visibility.

### Is SEO included in digital marketing?

Many digital marketing strategies include SEO as a foundational component.

### Do small businesses need digital marketing?

Yes. Digital marketing helps businesses compete more effectively and reach customers online.

### What is better: SEO or Google Ads?

Both serve different purposes. Many businesses benefit from a combination of long-term SEO and targeted advertising campaigns.

### Can digital marketing help local businesses?

Yes. Local SEO and location-based marketing can significantly improve visibility within a target service area.
