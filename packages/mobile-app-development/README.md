## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does every business need a mobile application?

Not every business requires a mobile application immediately. However, businesses with recurring customer interactions, bookings, transactions, or operational workflows can benefit significantly from mobile apps.

### Should businesses start with an MVP?

Yes. MVP (Minimum Viable Product) development allows businesses to validate ideas, gather feedback, and reduce initial investment risks.

### Can mobile applications be expanded later?

Yes. Mobile applications can be enhanced with additional modules, integrations, and features as business requirements grow.

### Which platform should businesses choose?

Many businesses start with Android or Cross-Platform solutions depending on budget, target audience, and business goals.

### Can mobile applications integrate with existing systems?

Yes. Applications can integrate with CRM systems, ERP platforms, payment gateways, customer portals, and third-party services.
