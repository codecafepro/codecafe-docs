# CodeCafe

## Build. Launch. Grow.

CodeCafe is a business technology platform dedicated to helping startups, SMEs, healthcare organizations, and growing businesses establish, optimize, and scale their digital presence.

From professional business websites to mobile applications, ERP solutions, digital marketing, customer portals, and business automation systems, CodeCafe provides practical solutions designed to solve real business challenges.

---

## What We Do

### Website Development

Professional business websites, corporate websites, e-commerce platforms, customer portals, and custom web solutions designed to improve credibility, lead generation, and business growth.

### Mobile Application Development

Android, iOS, and cross-platform mobile applications built to help businesses improve customer engagement and operational efficiency.

### ERP & Business Automation

Custom ERP systems, workflow automation, employee management solutions, CRM integrations, and operational platforms designed to streamline business processes.

### Digital Marketing

SEO, Google Ads, Social Media Marketing, Content Marketing, and Lead Generation strategies focused on helping businesses reach the right audience.

### Startup & Growth Solutions

Technology consulting, MVP development, digital transformation planning, and scalable solutions for startups and growing businesses.
