# Website Maintenance Checklist

## Why Website Maintenance Matters

Regular maintenance improves security, performance, and user experience.

## Monthly Checklist

### Security

* Check SSL Certificate
* Review Security Updates
* Backup Website

### Performance

* Speed Testing
* Broken Link Checks
* Database Optimization

### Content

* Review Business Information
* Update Services
* Add New Content

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often should websites be maintained?

Monthly reviews are recommended.

### Can outdated websites affect business performance?

Yes. Poor performance can impact user trust and search visibility.
