# Business Requirement Document (BRD) Guide

## What is a BRD?

A Business Requirement Document (BRD) defines business goals, requirements, processes, and expected outcomes for a project.

## Key Sections

### Business Objectives

Define the business problem.

### User Roles

Identify all user types.

### Functional Requirements

Describe what the system should do.

### Non-Functional Requirements

Performance, security, and scalability expectations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is a BRD important?

A BRD helps align business goals and technical implementation.

### Who should prepare the BRD?

Business owners, stakeholders, and implementation teams should collaborate.
